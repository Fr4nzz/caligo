## Changes made

Downloaded from the PLOS figure endpoint at `size=large` (2150 × 1599
PNG), trimmed of surrounding whitespace, resized to 1600 px wide and
re-encoded as JPEG at quality 86. No content was altered, cropped out or
relabelled.

A crop to the phylogeny panel alone was tried first and abandoned: the
tree, the chromosome-count boxes and the range maps interleave
horizontally, so no clean vertical cut keeps every `Chr` label without
also bleeding fragments of the maps.

## Why this figure

It is the published evidence for two claims the Science page makes in
prose — that haploid chromosome counts in this clade run from 21 to
between 56 and 60, and that three separate W–autosome fusions have been
identified. Both are readable directly off the figure: the grey `Chr`
boxes and the three dashed arrows labelled `W+4 fusion`, `W+9 fusion`
and `W+14 fusion`.
