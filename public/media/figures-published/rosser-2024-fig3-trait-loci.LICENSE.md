## Why this figure, and the honest caveat

This replaces a diagram Caligo drew. Our version showed 21 chromosome
bars with orange blocks marking introgressed regions — a paraphrase of
this figure with the measurements removed. Reproducing the real thing is
more honest and costs nothing, because the licence permits it.

It is, however, a research figure and not an explanatory one. A reader
who does not know what F<sub>ST</sub> is will not get anything from the
grey traces. What they *can* read directly, and what the caption points
them at, is the row of coloured bars above each chromosome and the
legend naming them: colour pattern, wing shape, host plant, flight,
pheromones, male preference. That is the claim the section makes —
introgressed regions are scattered across many chromosomes and they
carry the traits that keep the species apart.

It is rendered at full container width for this reason. Do not place it
in a narrow column; below roughly 900 px the chromosome labels stop
being readable and it becomes decoration.
