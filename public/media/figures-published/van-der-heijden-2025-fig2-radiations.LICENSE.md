## Why this figure

Figure 2 brings the paper's main result into one view. It combines the two
time-calibrated butterfly phylogenies with inferred hybridisation events,
wing patterns, geography, chromosome counts and elevation ranges. It is a
useful visual example of the different questions that reference genomes and
population data can address together.
